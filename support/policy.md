## Reporting a bug

**Please include the seed shown on the main screen.**

The seed decides every random roll in a run — the board, the enemies, and
the panels you get. With it we can replay your exact run on our side and see
the same thing you saw. Without it we usually cannot reproduce the problem,
and the report has to wait for the next occurrence.

A useful report contains:

- **The seed** (bottom-left of the main screen)
- What you did, and what happened instead
- Your device model and iOS version
- Whether the app was fully restarted afterwards

## Purchases and refunds

The game has one non-consumable in-app purchase: a one-time unlock of
chapters 2-6 and all 16 cats. It is not a subscription and does not renew.

- **Restoring a purchase:** open the unlock screen and tap
  **Restore Purchases**. If you reinstalled the app or changed devices, this
  brings your unlock back — it is tied to your store account, not to the
  device.
- **Refunds:** purchases are handled by Apple, so we cannot refund directly.
  Use Apple's [Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

If Restore Purchases does not find a purchase you made, tell us with the
email address on the purchase and we will look into it.

## Frequently asked

**Do I need an account?**
No. There is no sign-up and no login.

**Does the game need a network connection?**
Only to check whether an in-app purchase is owned. Everything else works
offline.

**Does the game collect any data?**
No. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).

**How do I unlock everything without paying?**
Play through chapter 1, which is free.
